# News search: "Quest Diagnostics" breach

## NBC News, 2026-10-22: "Quest Diagnostics says hackers accessed patient records in lab ordering system"
Quest said in a securities filing that an unauthorized party accessed certain patient records in its Quanum Lab Services Manager platform. The company did not say how many patients were affected or what kinds of information were involved. No ransomware or extortion group has claimed responsibility.

## HealthTech Daily, 2026-10-23: "Quest's latest breach revives memories of past incidents"
Quest has been here before: nearly 12 million Quest patients had data exposed in 2019 when its billing collections vendor, American Medical Collection Agency (AMCA), was breached. The scope of the new Quanum incident has not been disclosed.
