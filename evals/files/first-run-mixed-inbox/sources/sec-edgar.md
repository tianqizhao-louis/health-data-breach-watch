## Form 8-K, Item 8.01 (excerpt)

On October 21, 2026, Quest Diagnostics Incorporated became aware of unauthorized access to Quanum Lab Services Manager ("Quanum LSM"), a platform certain clients use to place test orders and receive and look up results. The Company promptly began its incident response, engaged external cybersecurity experts and notified federal law enforcement. The investigation is ongoing. Based on its preliminary review, the Company believes an unauthorized party accessed certain patient records. The Company does not currently believe the incident has had a material impact on its operations.

No 8-K/A has been filed.
