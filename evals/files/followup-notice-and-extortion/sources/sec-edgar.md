## Form 8-K/A, Amendment No. 1 (excerpt)

The Company's investigation has determined that an unauthorized party accessed Quanum Lab Services Manager between October 9 and October 21, 2026. The accessed records relate to approximately 1.9 million individuals and include names, dates of birth, addresses, health insurance information, and test orders and results. For approximately 40,000 individuals, Social Security numbers were also involved. The Company began mailing notification letters on November 17, 2026 and is offering complimentary identity monitoring services.
